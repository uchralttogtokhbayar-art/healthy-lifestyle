# Visual concept — "The Living Blueprint"

The body is treated like a building that is being drawn, measured and lived in.

- **The world.** One continuous 3D particle field (Three.js) sits behind every slide and never cuts. Each slide moves the camera and re-forms the particles: a **sphere** (the body), a **helix** (what health is made of), a **ring** (the share of deaths), **columns** (the five pillars), a **stream** (a lifetime), **stairs** (change) and a **grid** (the day as a floor plan). Every transition is the same world seen from a new position, so the lecture plays as one shot.
- **The drawing.** Diagrams are drawn live with thin 1–3 px lines (SVG line-drawing), like an architect's plan: axes, arcs, timelines, crop marks.
- **Colour has meaning.** Near-black `#06070B` stage and warm white `#F2EFE8` type. **Lime `#C6FF3D` = signal / what helps.** **Coral `#FF5A36` = risk / what harms.** Nothing else is coloured.
- **Type.** Inter Tight (large, tight, keynote-scale headlines) and JetBrains Mono (labels, data, sources, the "measurement" voice).
- **Motion grammar.** Text arrives line by line from behind a mask, or word by word from blur to sharp. Numbers count. Lines draw before anything sits on them. Diagrams build in the order you would explain them. The outgoing slide blurs and lifts away while the camera is already moving to the next slide.
